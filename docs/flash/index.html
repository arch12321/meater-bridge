📋 Instructions

Connect your ESP32

Plug your ESP32 into your computer with a USB data cable (not charge-only).

Select your board type

Choose the ESP32 variant that matches your hardware from the dropdown above.

Click "Connect & Install"

Select your ESP32's serial port when prompted, then wait for the flash to complete.

Configure Wi-Fi

After flashing, the ESP32 creates a MEATER-Bridge-xxxxxx Wi-Fi network. Connect to it, open 192.168.4.1, and log in as user admin with password Meater1234.

Flash not starting? Hold the BOOT button on your ESP32 while clicking "Connect & Install". Release it after the flash begins. On ESP32-C3, BOOT is GPIO 9. On original ESP32, it's GPIO 0.

🔧 What happens next?

After flashing:

  • The ESP32 starts as a Wi-Fi access point named MEATER-Bridge-xxxxxx
  • Connect to it and open 192.168.4.1 — log in as admin / Meater1234
  • Configure your home Wi-Fi + MQTT settings and save
  • The bridge connects to your MEATER probe/base over Bluetooth
  • Your MEATER app sees the probe over Wi-Fi (same network, no re-pairing)
  • Home Assistant auto-discovers 44 entities via MQTT

See the full documentation for setup details.
